Facial Bones AP

AP Axial / Reverse Waters Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The AP Facial Bones projection (often performed as a Reverse Waters view) is used primarily for trauma patients to evaluate fractures of the facial bones, orbits, maxillae, and zygomatic arches.

Body Part Facial Bones / Orbits / Maxilla
Projection AP Axial (Reverse Waters)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75 - 80
mAs 20 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient supine on radiographic table with neck extended if possible.

• Adjust head so MML (Mentomeatal Line) is perpendicular to IR plane.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) aligned perpendicular to midline of table/IR.

• If patient cannot extend neck, angle CR parallel to MML.

• Ensure no rotation or tilt of the skull.

Central Ray

• CR directed perpendicular to IR (parallel to MML) entering at the acanthion.

• If neck flexion is restricted, angle CR cephalic to align parallel with MML.

• Center CR to MSP at level of acanthion.

• Collimate closely to facial region.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Maxillary sinuses and facial structure clearly demonstrated.

✓ Petrous ridges projected inferior to floor of maxillary sinuses.

✓ Distance between lateral orbital margins and outer skull walls equal on both sides (no rotation).

✓ Orbital rims and zygomatic arches visualized clearly.

✓ Sharp bony detail of midface structures.
