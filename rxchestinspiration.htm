Chest PA Inspiration

Standard Posteroanterior Chest Radiographic Projection (Full Inspiration)

Position Images

Examination Information

The standard PA inspiration chest radiograph demonstrates the lungs, heart, vascular structures, mediastinum, and thoracic cage. Exposure is made on full inspiration to maximize lung expansion and clear visualization of pulmonary pathology.

Body Part Chest / Thorax
Projection Posteroanterior (PA) Inspiration
IR Size 35 × 43 cm
SID 180 cm
kVp 110 – 125
mAs 4 – 8

Patient Position

• Patient standing erect facing the vertical grid / bucky.

• Chin elevated and rested on top of IR support.

• Hands placed on lower hips with palms facing outward, elbows pushed forward to roll scapulae out of lung fields.

• Mid-sagittal plane (MSP) centered perpendicular to IR.

• Exposure made on full inspiration (end of second deep breath).

Central Ray

• Central Ray perpendicular to IR.

• Center Central Ray to level of T7 (approx. 18–20 cm below vertebra prominens or level of inferior border of scapulae).

• Center IR to CR (top of IR approx. 4–5 cm above shoulders).

• Collimate on four sides to outer margins of chest walls.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire lung fields included from apices down to costophrenic angles.

✓ Full inspiration demonstrated: 10 posterior ribs visible above diaphragm.

✓ No rotation: sternoclavicular joints equidistant from vertebral spine.

✓ Scapulae projected outside of the lung fields.

✓ Sharp vascular markings and faint outline of thoracic vertebrae behind heart shadow.
