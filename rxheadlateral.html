Lateral Skull

Lateral Skull Radiographic Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The Lateral skull projection demonstrates the cranial bones, sella turcica, anterior and posterior clinoid processes, and facial structure profiles.

Body Part Skull
Projection Lateral
IR Size 24 × 30 cm (Crosswise)
SID 100 cm
kVp 65 - 70
mAs 12 - 15

Patient Position

• Patient seated erect or semiprone with body rotated for comfort.

• Place side of head closest to IR (affected side down).

• Interpupillary line (IPL) perpendicular to IR.

• Infraorbitomeatal line (IOML) parallel to transverse border of IR.

• Mid-sagittal plane (MSP) parallel to IR plane.

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IR.

• Center CR 2 inches (5 cm) superior to External Auditory Meatus (EAM).

• Collimate on four sides to cover entire cranium.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire cranium displayed in profile.

✓ Superimposed orbital roofs and greater wings of sphenoid.

✓ Superimposed mandibular rami and temporomandibular joints.

✓ Sella turcica in profile without rotation.

✓ Sharp bony boundaries and clear detail of cranial bones.
