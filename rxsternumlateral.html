Sternum Lateral Position

Lateral Radiographic Projection for Sternal Fractures, Displacements & Pathologies

Position Images

Examination Information

The Lateral Sternum projection provides a true side profile view of the sternum to demonstrate anterior or posterior displacement of fractures, sternal depressed lesions, and localized pathology free of rib and cardiac superimposition.

Body Part Sternum / Anterior Thoracic Wall
Projection Lateral Projection
IR Size 24 × 30 cm (Lengthwise)
SID 180 cm (72 inches)
kVp 75 – 85
mAs 20 – 35

Patient Position

• Patient positioned standing or sitting upright facing laterally against vertical bucky/grid (erect position preferred to prevent sag).

• Place patient's lateral chest wall in contact with vertical IR.

• Instruct patient to draw shoulders and arms back fully behind body, clasping hands behind lower back to clear humeri from sternum.

• Elevate chin slightly and adjust chest so sternum is perpendicular to IR plane.

• Respiration: Deep inspiration (draws lungs full of air and projects sternum anteriorly away from cardiac silhouette). Suspended inspiration during exposure.

Central Ray

• Central Ray horizontal and perpendicular to Image Receptor.

• Center Central Ray to mid-sternum (midway between jugular notch and xiphoid process, approximately level of T7).

• Center IR to CR (top of IR located ~4 cm above jugular notch).

• Collimate tightly to anterior-posterior borders of the sternum.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire sternum (manubrium, sternal body, and xiphoid process) clearly demonstrated in true profile.

✓ No overlap or superimposition of the sternum by soft tissue of shoulders or humeri.

✓ Sternum projected anteriorly away from ribs and heart.

✓ Sharp detail of cortical borders and absence of patient motion blur.
