Lateral Chest

Lateral Chest Radiographic Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The lateral chest projection serves as a complementary view to the PA chest, evaluating the retrosternal and retrocardiac spaces, posterior costophrenic angles, and lesions located behind the heart or diaphragm.

Body Part Chest / Thorax
Projection Lateral (Left Lateral Preferred)
IR Size 35 × 43 cm
SID 180 cm
kVp 110 – 125
mAs 6 – 10

Patient Position

• Patient standing upright with left side against vertical IR (Left Lateral decreases cardiac magnification).

• Arms raised above head and hands resting together over head or grabbing support bar.

• Midcoronal plane (MCP) perpendicular to IR and centered to midline of IR.

• True lateral stance with no rotation or tilt of shoulders and hips.

• Exposure made at the end of second full inspiration.

Central Ray

• Central ray perpendicular to IR.

• Center Central Ray to midcoronal plane at level of T7 (8–10 cm below inferior angle of scapula).

• Collimate to include anterior sternum and posterior ribs.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire lung fields demonstrated from apices to posterior costophrenic angles.

✓ Superimposition of posterior ribs (gap between posterior ribs < 1 cm indicates minimal rotation).

✓ Sternum demonstrated in profile without rotation.

✓ Arms and soft tissue cleared from upper lung fields.

✓ Clear visibility of retrosternal and retrocardiac air spaces.
