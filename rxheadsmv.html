Submentovertex (SMV)

Submentovertex Cranial Projection (Schüller Method)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Submentovertex (SMV) projection demonstrates the base of the skull, sphenoid and ethmoid sinuses, petrous pyramids, mandible, and zygomatic arches.

Body Part Skull Base / Sinuses / Zygomatic Arches
Projection Submentovertex (SMV)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75 - 80
mAs 20 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient seated erect or supine (with pillows under torso to allow full extension).

• Hyperextend neck until vertex of skull rests against IR surface.

• IOML (Infraorbitomeatal Line) parallel to IR plane.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline.

• Ensure no rotation or tilt of head.

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IOML.

• Center CR 1.5 inches (3.8 cm) inferior to mandibular symphysis (midway between angles of mandible).

• Collimate on four sides to include skull base boundaries.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Foramen magnum and cranial base structures clearly demonstrated.

✓ Mandibular condyles projected anterior to petrous ridges.

✓ Symphysis of mandible superimposed over frontal bone.

✓ Symmetric petrous pyramids and zygomatic arches (indicating no rotation).

✓ Sharp bony detail of sphenoid sinuses and skull base.
