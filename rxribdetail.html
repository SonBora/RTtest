Rib Detail (Localized Rib View)

Targeted High-Resolution Radiographic Projection for Specific Rib Lesions & Fractures

Position Images

Examination Information

The localized rib detail projection utilizes tight collimation and optimal exposure techniques focused directly over the point of painful clinical interest. It evaluates subtle non-displaced cortical fractures, lytic/sclerotic bony lesions, or focal trauma in specific rib segments.

Body Part Ribs / Localized Chest Wall
Projection Localized AP / PA / Tangential Rib Detail
IR Size 18 × 24 cm or 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 65 – 75
mAs 8 – 16

Patient Position

• Patient placed in erect or recumbent position (erect preferred for ribs above diaphragm; supine/prone for below diaphragm).

• Place patient's area of maximal tenderness directly in contact with or centered to the IR.

• Adjust body rotation (AP, PA, or oblique angle) so the specific rib segment lies parallel to the IR or in profile.

• Use radiopaque marker or BB marker over the exact point of patient's reported pain if indicated.

• Respiration: Full inspiration for upper ribs; full expiration for lower ribs. Suspended respiration during exposure.

Central Ray

• Central Ray perpendicular to Image Receptor (or angled tangentially if evaluating rib profile).

• Center Central Ray directly to the point of localized pain / interest.

• Center IR to CR.

• Tightly collimate to include only the localized area of interest (improves contrast and reduces scatter radiation).

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Targeted rib segment demonstrated in high contrast and maximum spatial resolution.

✓ Tight collimation margins clearly visible surrounding the localized region of interest.

✓ Sharp demonstration of trabecular pattern and subtle cortical margins without motion artifact.

✓ Anatomical side marker and pain localization marker (if used) clearly visible within collimated field.
