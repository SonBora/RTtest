PA Chest

Posteroanterior Chest Radiographic Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The PA chest projection is the standard projection used to evaluate the lungs, mediastinum, heart, and thoracic bony structures.

Body Part Chest / Thorax
Projection Posteroanterior (PA)
IR Size 35 × 43 cm
SID 180 cm
kVp 110 – 125
mAs 3 – 5

Patient Position

• Patient standing upright with anterior chest placed flat against vertical IR.

• Chin elevated and resting on top of IR.

• Hands on lower hips with palms facing outward, shoulders rolled forward to rotate scapulae out of lung fields.

• MSP perpendicular to midline of IR.

• Exposure made on full inspiration (end of second deep breath).

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IR.

• Center Central Ray to level of T7 (18–20 cm below vertebra prominens).

• Collimate to outer borders of chest walls.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Both lung fields demonstrated from apices to costophrenic angles.

✓ Scapulae projected outside lung fields.

✓ No rotation: sternoclavicular joints equidistant from spine.

✓ Minimum 10 posterior ribs visible above diaphragm.

✓ Sharp heart shadow and diaphragmatic contours with visible vascularity.
