AP Chest

Anteroposterior Chest Radiographic Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The AP chest projection demonstrates the lungs, heart, and thoracic structures. It is primarily used for bedridden or supine patients who cannot tolerate a PA standing projection.

Body Part Chest / Thorax
Projection Anteroposterior (AP)
IR Size 35 × 43 cm
SID 100 cm (or 180 cm if possible)
kVp 85 – 110
mAs 3 – 5

Patient Position

• Patient seated erect or supine on table/bed with back resting against IR.

• Posterior aspect of shoulders and chest aligned parallel with IR.

• Roll shoulders forward to help clear scapulae from lung fields as much as possible.

• MSP perpendicular to midline of IR.

• Exposure made at the end of second full inspiration.

Central Ray

• Central ray angled caudad 3–5° perpendicular to long axis of sternum (or perpendicular to IR).

• Center Central Ray to enter 8–10 cm below jugular notch (level of T7).

• Collimate to outer margins of chest fields.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Both lung fields included from apices to costophrenic angles.

✓ No rotation: sternoclavicular joints equidistant from spine.

✓ Clavicles appear more horizontal compared to PA projection.

✓ Minimum 8–10 posterior ribs visible above diaphragm.

✓ Sharp bony detail and visible pulmonary vascular structures.
