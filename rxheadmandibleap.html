Mandible AP / PA

Posteroanterior / Anteroposterior Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The Mandible AP / PA projection demonstrates fractures or structural abnormalities of the mandibular body, rami, and condyloid processes, as well as symmetry of the lower jaw.

Body Part Mandible Body / Rami / Condyles
Projection PA / AP Projection
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 70 - 80
mAs 15 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient seated erect or prone/supine with forehead and nose against IR surface (PA position).

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to IR plane.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline to prevent rotation.

• Rest chin on IR for PA projection or extend neck slightly depending on clinical interest.

• Immobilize head to avoid motion artifacts.

Central Ray

• CR directed perpendicular to IR (or angled 20°–25° cephalad for PA axial rami evaluation).

• Center CR to exit at the level of the lips (acanthion / junction of lips).

• Center IR to CR.

• Collimate on four sides to include the entire mandible from temporomandibular joints to symphysis.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire mandible from condyles to mental symphysis fully included.

✓ Symmetric appearance of mandibular body and rami on both sides (no head rotation).

✓ Mandibular body and rami visualized with minimal superimposition of cervical spine.

✓ Condyloid processes clearly demonstrated.

✓ Sharp bony detail showing clear cortical margins and trabecular pattern.
