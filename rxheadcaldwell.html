Caldwell View

PA Axial Projection 15° Caudal (Caldwell Method)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Caldwell projection demonstrates the frontal sinuses, anterior ethmoid air cells, superior orbital margins, and petrous ridges in the lower third of the orbits.

Body Part Facial Bones / Sinuses / Skull
Projection PA Axial 15° Caudal (Caldwell)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75 - 80
mAs 20 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient facing the image receptor (erect or prone).

• Place forehead and nose against IR surface.

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to IR plane.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline.

• Ensure no head rotation or tilt.

Central Ray

• Angle CR 15° caudal to exit at the nasion.

• Center Central Ray to IR center.

• Collimate on four sides to include skull and facial boundaries.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Frontal sinuses and anterior ethmoid cells clearly demonstrated.

✓ Petrous ridges projected into lower one-third of orbits.

✓ No rotation of cranium (equal distance from lateral orbital borders to lateral skull walls).

✓ Superior orbital margins and crista galli clearly visualized.

✓ Sharp bony detail and clear visualization of sinus aeration.
