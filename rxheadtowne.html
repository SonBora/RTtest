Towne View

AP Axial Projection 30° Caudal (Towne Method)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Towne projection demonstrates the occipital bone, petrous pyramids, dorsum sellae, and posterior clinoid processes visualized within the foramen magnum.

Body Part Skull / Occipital Bone
Projection AP Axial 30° Caudal (Towne)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75 - 80
mAs 20 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient supine or seated erect with back toward IR.

• Posterior aspect of head (occiput) rests against IR.

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to IR (or IOML perpendicular if neck flexion is limited).

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline.

• Ensure no rotation or tilt of the head.

Central Ray

• Angle CR 30° caudal to OML (or 37° caudal to IOML).

• Center CR 2.5 inches (6.5 cm) superior to glabella to exit at the foramen magnum.

• Collimate to include the entire cranial vault and occipital region.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Occipital bone and petrous pyramids clearly demonstrated.

✓ Dorsum sellae and posterior clinoid processes projected inside the foramen magnum.

✓ Symmetric petrous ridges (indicating no rotation of head).

✓ Equal distance from lateral border of foramen magnum to lateral margins of skull.

✓ Sharp bony detail of posterior cranial structures.
