AP Skull

Anteroposterior Skull Radiographic Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The AP skull projection demonstrates the frontal bone, orbits, and cranial vault. It is often performed when the patient cannot be placed in a PA position.

Body Part Skull
Projection Anteroposterior (AP)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75
mAs 20

Patient Position

• Patient supine or seated erect with back toward IR.

• Posterior aspect of head (occiput) against IR.

• MSP perpendicular to midline of IR.

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to IR.

• Remove metallic objects (earrings, hairpins, glasses) from head.

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IR.

• Center Central Ray to enter at nasion.

• Collimate to include entire cranial vault.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire cranial vault included.

✓ No rotation or tilt of skull (symmetric lateral borders).

✓ Symmetric orbital margins and petrous ridges.

✓ Petrous ridges fill orbits.

✓ Sharp bony detail and visible trabecular structure.
