Orbital Floor View

Modified Waters Method (Modified Parietoacanthial Projection)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Orbital Floor projection (Modified Waters method) is primarily used to evaluate blowout fractures, orbital floor integrity, inferior orbital margins, and foreign body localization within the orbit.

Body Part Orbital Floor / Orbits / Maxillae
Projection Modified Waters (Parietoacanthial)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 70 - 75
mAs 15 - 20

Patient Position

• Patient seated erect or prone with chin and nose resting against IR surface.

• Adjust head position so LML (Lips-Meatal Line) is perpendicular to IR.

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) forms an angle of approximately 55° with IR plane.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline.

• Ensure no rotation or tilt of the head.

Central Ray

• CR directed perpendicular to IR.

• Center CR to exit at the acanthion (midway between orbits).

• Center IR to CR.

• Collimate closely to include both orbital regions and maxillae.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Orbital floor clearly demonstrated perpendicular to IR without superimposition.

✓ Petrous pyramids projected into lower half of maxillary sinuses (below orbital floors).

✓ Equal distance from lateral orbital wall to outer margin of skull on both sides (no rotation).

✓ Inferior orbital rims, zygomatic arches, and maxillae clearly demonstrated.

✓ Sharp detail of orbital bones and soft tissue boundaries.
