Reverse Towne View

PA Axial Projection 25° Cephalic (Haas Method)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Reverse Towne (Haas method) demonstrates the occipital bone, petrous pyramids, dorsum sellae, and posterior clinoid processes projected inside the foramen magnum with reduced thyroid/facial exposure.

Body Part Skull / Occipital Bone
Projection PA Axial 25° Cephalic (Haas)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75 - 80
mAs 20 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient prone or seated erect facing IR/Bucky.

• Rest forehead and nose against IR surface.

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to IR.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline.

• Ensure no rotation or tilt of head.

Central Ray

• Angle CR 25° cephalic to OML.

• Center CR to MSP entering 1.5 inches (3.8 cm) inferior to external occipital protuberance (inion) to exit at glabella.

• Collimate to include occipital region and cranial boundaries.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Occipital region and petrous pyramids clearly demonstrated.

✓ Dorsum sellae and posterior clinoid processes projected inside foramen magnum.

✓ Symmetric petrous ridges on both sides of skull (indicating no rotation).

✓ Equal distance from lateral margin of foramen magnum to outer cranial margin.

✓ Sharp bony detail of posterior skull structures.
