AP Lordotic Chest

Anteroposterior Lordotic Chest Projection (Lindblom Method)

Position Images

Examination Information

The AP lordotic chest projection project the clavicles above the lung apices. It is specifically utilized to rule out or evaluate pathology and lesions in the apical regions of the lungs.

Body Part Chest / Lung Apices
Projection AP Axial / Lordotic (Lindblom Method)
IR Size 35 × 43 cm
SID 180 cm
kVp 110 – 125
mAs 4 – 6

Patient Position

• Patient standing approx. 30 cm (1 foot) away from IR, facing x-ray tube.

• Lean backward with shoulders, neck, and back resting against upper IR (Lordotic stance).

• Place hands on lower hips, roll shoulders forward to move scapulae out of lung fields.

• MSP perpendicular to midline of IR.

• Exposure made on end of second full inspiration.

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IR (if patient leans back into full lordotic posture).

• Alternative (AP Axial): If patient cannot lean, angle CR 15–20° cephalad.

• Center Central Ray to mid-sternum (level of T7 / 9 cm below jugular notch).

• Collimate to include lung apices and upper thoracic fields.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Clavicles projected superior to lung apices.

✓ Medial ends of clavicles superimposed on 1st or 2nd ribs.

✓ Ribs appear distorted with anterior and posterior parts superimposed.

✓ Entire lung fields included, especially apical areas clearly visualized.

✓ No rotation: sternoclavicular joints equidistant from vertebral column.
