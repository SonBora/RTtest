Facial Bones Lateral

Lateral Projection (Side of Interest)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Lateral Facial Bones projection is used to demonstrate fractures of the facial bones, orbital roofs, nasal bones, and maxillae, as well as foreign body localization.

Body Part Facial Bones / Zygoma / Orbits
Projection Lateral (Right or Left)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 65 - 75
mAs 10 - 15

Patient Position

• Patient erect or semi-prone with affected side of face against IR.

• Adjust head into a true lateral position.

• IPL (Interpupillary Line) perpendicular to IR plane.

• IOML (Infraorbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to front edge of IR.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) parallel to IR plane.

Central Ray

• CR directed perpendicular to IR.

• Center CR to zygoma (midway between outer canthus and EAM).

• Center IR to CR.

• Collimate on four sides to include all facial bones.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Superimposed orbital roofs and mandibular rami (indicating no tilt or rotation).

✓ Superimposed sella turcica.

✓ All facial bones demonstrated from supraorbital margin to mandibular angle.

✓ Zygoma positioned in center of radiograph.

✓ Sharp bony and soft tissue detail of facial structures.
