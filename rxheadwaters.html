Waters View

Parietoacanthial Projection (Waters Method)

Position Images

Examination Information

The Waters projection demonstrates the maxillary sinuses, facial bones, orbits, and zygomatic arches with minimal bony overlap.

Body Part Facial Bones / Sinuses
Projection Parietoacanthial (Waters)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75 - 80
mAs 20 - 25

Patient Position

• Patient facing the image receptor (upright or prone).

• Extend neck and place chin against IR surface.

• MML (Mentomeatal Line) perpendicular to IR plane.

• OML (Orbitomeatal Line) forms a 37° angle with the IR plane.

• MSP (Mid-sagittal Plane) perpendicular to IR midline.

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IR.

• Center Central Ray to exit at the acanthion.

• Collimate to include all facial bones and maxillary sinuses.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Maxillary sinuses clearly demonstrated without petrous overlap.

✓ Petrous ridges projected completely inferior to maxillary sinuses.

✓ No rotation of facial bones (equal distance from outer lateral border to orbits).

✓ Orbits, zygomatic arches, and facial bones well visualized.

✓ Sharp bony margins and clear sinus aeration detail.
