Nasal Bones

Lateral Projection (Bilateral) & Waters View

Position Images

Examination Information

The Nasal Bones projection is performed to evaluate fractures or displacement of the nasal bones, nasal septum medial deviation, and frontonasal suture integrity following facial trauma.

Body Part Nasal Bones / Septum / Frontonasal Suture
Projection Lateral (Both Sides) & Parietoacanthial (Waters)
IR Size 18 × 24 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 50 - 60 (Low kVp for low density structures)
mAs 5 - 8

Patient Position

• Patient seated erect or semi-prone with side of face against IR.

• Adjust head into a true lateral position with MSP parallel to IR plane.

• IPL (Interpupillary Line) perpendicular to IR plane.

• IOML (Infraorbitomeatal Line) perpendicular to front edge of IR.

• Perform both Right and Left Lateral projections for comparison.

Central Ray

• CR directed perpendicular to IR.

• Center CR to 0.5 inch (1.25 cm) inferior to the nasion.

• Center IR to CR.

• Collimate tightly to include nasal bones and soft tissue profile.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Nasal bone and soft tissue structures demonstrated without superimposition.

✓ Frontonasal suture and anterior nasal spine visualized.

✓ No tilt or rotation of nasal structures demonstrated.

✓ Optimal low-contrast technique showing both thin nasal bones and soft tissue contour.

✓ Both Right and Left lateral projections labeled correctly for comparison.
