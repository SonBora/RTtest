Posteroanterior Skull Radiographic Projection

Position Images

Examination Information

The PA skull projection demonstrates the cranial vault, facial bones, and orbital structures.

Body Part Skull
Projection Posteroanterior (PA)
IR Size 24 × 30 cm
SID 100 cm
kVp 75
mAs 20

Patient Position

• Patient facing the image receptor.

• Forehead and nose placed against IR.

• MSP perpendicular to detector.

• OML perpendicular to IR.

• Remove metallic objects from head area.

Central Ray

• CR perpendicular to IR.

• Center at the nasion.

• Collimate to include entire skull.

Image Evaluation Criteria

✓ Entire skull included.

✓ No rotation of head.

✓ Symmetric orbital margins.

✓ Petrous ridges demonstrated.

✓ Sharp bony detail.
